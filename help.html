Help

Viewport Controls:

Zoom – Scroll Wheel

Rotate – Hold left mouse button and drag

Pan – Hold right mouse button and pan

The Viewport Gizmo on the bottom right can also be utilized to rotate the scene and snap to axises.

Inverse Kinematics Controls:

Click and drag the green tori to bend and pose the fingers.

Gizmo Controls:

Use the left panel to change the type of gizmo, and click on the red dots to select the joint to transform. Default is Move. Only use the gizmos to make minor adjustments to the pose, as they deform the hand.

[1] — Move

[2] — Rotate

Top Toolbar:

Buttons in order: Undo, Redo, Screenshot Pose, Help Doc.
